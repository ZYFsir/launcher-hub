# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion focused on the project.
Harassment, discrimination, and disclosure of other people's private data are
not acceptable.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately. Maintainers may remove
comments, close issues, or block participants to protect the community.
